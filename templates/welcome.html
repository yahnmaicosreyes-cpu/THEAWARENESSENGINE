🔐 Password-Protected App

This app is secured with HTTP Basic Auth. There is no logout button — your browser keeps you signed in until you explicitly clear your credentials or close the browser session. To sign out, use one of the steps below:

  • Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy & security → Clear browsing data → check Passwords and other sign-in data → Clear now
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Logins and Passwords → Saved Logins → find this site and remove it
  • Safari: Settings → Passwords → find this site and delete it

Clicking Login will open a credentials prompt from your browser.
